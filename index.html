The Format

How every session runs

The same rhythm every Monday and Wednesday, so players always know what's next — but each day has its own fresh set of stations. Our 45 players split into 4 groups and rotate through them together.

10mins

Ball Mastery

Every player, a ball each. Juggling and ball-skill moves to warm up feet and eyes before we get to work.

Same start every session
50mins

Stations

Four stations built around the week's topic. Each takes a whole group (~11 players) and is set up for maximum touches — a ball each or several small groups working at once. Any drill with a line runs as several short lines of no more than 3, so nobody stands and waits. A parent-coach or two run each station and stay put while the groups rotate every 12 minutes.

4 × 12 min · ~11 per grid · built for max touches
15mins

Small-Sided Games

We finish with four small-sided games at once — each group plays on its own grid, 5v5 or 6v6 depending on who's there. Real play where the day's skill shows up, and every child is always involved.

4 grids · 5v5 or 6v6

Station Rotation every 12 min · same all season

For Parents

Before you come

A few things that keep every session running smoothly for the little ones.

What to bring

  • Soccer ball (labeled with your child's name)
  • Shin guards
  • Cleats
  • Training uniform
  • Water bottle

Getting there

Please arrive 5–10 minutes early so we can start Ball Mastery on time. Walk your player to their group's coach and we'll take it from there.

Cheer them on

The best sideline support at this age is simple: encourage effort, celebrate goals for both teams, and let the coaches coach. Every child plays every session.
